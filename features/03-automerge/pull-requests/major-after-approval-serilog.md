## What happened and why

The configuration has two levels of control for a major update:

| Step | Who acts | Controlled by |
|---|---|---|
| Create the branch and the PR | A person selects the checkbox in the Dependency Dashboard | `dependencyDashboardApproval: true` |
| Merge the PR | A person reviews and merges | `automerge` is not set for major updates |

The approval rule stops Renovate before the branch exists. No CI runs and no PR notification goes out for a major update that nobody wants now. When the team is ready, one click starts the normal PR flow.

The approval does not merge the PR. The body says `Disabled by config` for automerge, because only the patch rule sets `automerge: true`.
